How to read

One cloud for all three sources. Dining, Tech and Film are the corners of an upside-down triangle. Each word starts at the average of the corners, weighted by how many sentences of each source contain it, then spirals outwards until it finds space. Words every source uses sit in the middle; words one source uses alone sit in its corner. Colour still shows praise or complaint, over all 3,000 sentences.

Size is the number of sentences that contain the word, on one square-root scale. Colour: green at 70% or more, pink at 30% or less (in this data that roughly matches statistical significance), grey between. Italic terms are "not …" phrases counted as one term.

Shown: every term found in at least 7 sentences of one source. Grammar words (the, of, is, they …) are removed. Hover a word for its counts per source and one real sentence.

Data: Sentiment Labelled Sentences, Kotzias et al., KDD 2015 (UCI Machine Learning Repository).
