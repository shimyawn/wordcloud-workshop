How to read

One cloud per source. The largest words are placed first, at the centre, and every other word spirals outwards until it finds space. Position only packs the words; colour shows whether a word leans to praise or to complaint. The share of praise sentences adds five imaginary sentences on each side, so a word seen three times cannot jump to 0% or 100%; the dataset is built 500 : 500, so 50% means "used equally in both".

Size is the number of sentences that contain the word, on one square-root scale shared by all three clouds. Colour: green at 70% or more, pink at 30% or less (in this data that roughly matches statistical significance), grey between. Italic terms are "not …" phrases counted as one term.

Shown: the N terms of each source found in the most sentences (ties in alphabetical order). The note under each cloud shows the count at rank N and how many terms share it: a top N draws a line through tied words.

Data: Sentiment Labelled Sentences, Kotzias et al., KDD 2015 (UCI Machine Learning Repository).
