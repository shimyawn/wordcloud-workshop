Student guide · Visualize Text Data

Build a word cloud that shows which words people use to praise and which to complain

做一个词云：看人们用哪些词称赞，用哪些词抱怨

You will turn 3,000 review sentences into one word cloud per source. Size shows how often a word appears; colour shows whether it is used to praise or to complain. You build it in six steps with Copilot, check each step against a number, then change it in two ways by hand: how many words it shows, and what position means.

你将把 3,000 条评论句子做成每个来源一个词云。字号表示词出现的频率；颜色表示这个词更多用于称赞还是抱怨。你会用 Copilot 分六步完成，每一步都用一个数字核对，然后手动做两处修改：显示多少个词，以及位置代表什么。

1What a word cloud encodes词云编码了什么

In a word cloud the mark is the word itself, and the main channel is size: bigger means more often. You see the whole text at a glance, and large or bright words pop out before you search for them.

It also has three blind spots:

  • Position means nothing. The most effective channel, position, is spent on packing words wherever they fit.
  • Long words look more important. "excellent" covers more area than "bad" at the same count.
  • Cut words leave no trace. Most words are rare and must be left out, and the reader cannot see how many.

You will build a classic cloud: the biggest words in the centre, colour for tone. In Task 2 you give position a meaning, so each word leans towards the side it is used on: a second, redundant channel for the same attribute as colour.

在词云中，标记（mark）就是词本身，主要的通道（channel）是大小：越大表示出现越多。你可以一眼看到整个文本，又大又亮的词会在你寻找之前就跳出来（popout）。

它也有三个盲点：

  • 位置没有意义。最有效的通道——位置——被用来把词塞进空隙。
  • 长词看起来更重要。同样次数下，"excellent" 比 "bad" 占更大面积。
  • 被删掉的词不留痕迹。大多数词都很少见，必须删掉，而读者看不到删了多少。

你要做的是一个经典词云：大词在中间，用颜色表示语气。在任务 2 中你会让位置有意义，使每个词偏向它被使用的那一边：位置成为与颜色表达同一属性的冗余编码（redundant encoding）。

Find out yourself

自己找答案

What separates the sentence from its label?句子和标签之间用什么分隔？

A tab. Split on the last tab of the line.

一个制表符。按每行最后一个制表符切分。

Why does 50% mean "used equally in both"?为什么 50% 表示"两边用得一样多"？

Every source is exactly 500 praise and 500 complaint sentences, so the midpoint is a true neutral point.

每个来源恰好 500 句称赞、500 句抱怨，所以中点就是真正的中性点。

Can one sentence tell you whether a word is positive?一句话能说明一个词是正面的吗？

No. The label belongs to the whole sentence. You need many sentences, counted together.

不能。标签属于整句话，需要把许多句子合起来统计。

How many distinct words appear only once?有多少不同的词只出现一次？

About 58% of 5,069. This long tail is why every word cloud must leave words out.

5,069 个词中约 58%。这条长尾（long tail）就是每个词云都必须舍弃一些词的原因。

3Concepts概念

From a sentence to terms

从句子到词项

SentenceThe food isn't very good.
Tokensthe · food · is · not · very · good
Termsfood · not good
  • Tokenization: lowercase, split contractions (isn't → is not; careful with can't and won't), keep letters only.
  • Stop words: a written-out list of grammar words (the, of, is, they) that are dropped.
  • Negation: not, no and never join the next real word into one term; very in between is skipped.
  • Count sentences, not words: a term counts once per sentence, because the label belongs to the sentence.
  • 分词（tokenization）：转小写，拆开缩写（isn't → is not；注意 can't 和 won't），只保留字母。
  • 停用词（stop words）：一份写明的语法词列表（the, of, is, they），统计前删除。
  • 否定处理：not、no、never 与后面第一个实词合成一个词项；中间的 very 跳过。
  • 按句计数：一个词项在一句话里只算一次，因为标签属于整句。

Smoothed share of praise

平滑后的称赞比例

share = (praise + 5) ÷ (praise + complaint + 10)

Like a rating that needs enough reviews: five imaginary sentences on each side keep a word seen three times from jumping to 100%.

就像评分需要足够多的评价：两边各加 5 个"虚拟句子"，让只出现 3 次的词不会直接跳到 100%。

试一试

三种语气：发散型配色

The data has a meaningful midpoint (50%), so a neutral grey sits there and saturated colours at the ends. A word needs at least 7 one-sided sentences to reach 70%: try 6 / 0 and 7 / 0 above.

数据有一个有意义的中点（50%），所以中点用中性灰，两端用饱和色。一个词至少需要 7 句同一边的句子才能达到 70%：试试上面的 6 / 0 和 7 / 0。

Size and layout

字号与布局

  • Square-root scale. Area grows with the square of font size, so a √ scale keeps a word's area proportional to its count. Use one scale for all three clouds (a common scale), so they can be compared.
  • Spiral layout. Place the largest word first at its target, then walk out along a spiral until the word's box overlaps nothing. No randomness: everyone gets the same picture.
  • Small multiples. One cloud per source, same encoding, stacked.
  • 平方根比例尺。面积随字号的平方增长，所以用 √ 比例尺让词的面积与次数成正比。三个词云共用一个比例尺（共同尺度），才能互相比较。
  • 螺旋布局。最大的词先放在目标位置，其余的词沿螺旋向外走，直到不与任何词重叠。不用随机数：每个人得到相同的图。
  • 小多图（small multiples）。每个来源一个词云，编码相同，上下排列。

6Task 2 · Give position a meaning任务 2 · 让位置有意义

Objective

目标

Use the most effective channel, position, for the most important attribute, the share of praise. Keep one cloud: only each word's starting point moves.

把最有效的通道——位置——用于最重要的属性：称赞比例。仍然保持一个词云：只移动每个词的起点。

Requirements

要求

  1. Add drift to CONFIG (try 0.6).
  2. In layoutCloud, change the target: centre.x + drift × (share − 0.5) × width.
  3. Add an axis above the clouds: complaint ← → praise, ticks at 0–100% placed with the same formula.
  1. 在 CONFIG 中加入 drift（试试 0.6）。
  2. 在 layoutCloud 中修改目标：centre.x + drift × (比例 − 0.5) × 宽度。
  3. 在词云上方加一条坐标轴：抱怨 ← → 称赞，0–100% 的刻度用同一个公式定位。

Where

位置

CONFIG · step 5 (one line) · step 6

Look for target · centre.x · selectAll('text')

Hint提示

With drift = 1 the words reach the edges and the cloud breaks into islands; with 0 nothing changes. Somewhere between keeps a cloud and shows a lean.

drift = 1 时词会跑到边缘，词云裂成几块；drift = 0 时没有变化。取中间的值，既保持一个词云，又能看出偏向。

A slider for drift, from 0 to 1, that redraws the clouds with a transition.

加一个 drift 滑块（0 到 1），拖动时用过渡动画重画词云。

Troubleshooting

常见问题

The data does not load
Open the folder with Live Server, and check the folder name is data in lower case.
"d3 is not defined"
The D3 script tag must come before wordcloud.js in index.html.
Words overlap
Measure the width with the same font, size and weight you draw with, and wait for the web font to load first.
Numbers are a little off
Check the contraction rules (can't, won't) and that a term counts once per sentence.
Top 1000 is slow
Check the overlap test only looks at nearby words (the grid in step 5).
数据加载不出来
用 Live Server 打开文件夹，并确认文件夹名是小写的 data。
"d3 is not defined"
index.html 里 D3 的 script 标签必须在 wordcloud.js 之前。
词互相重叠
测量宽度时使用与绘制相同的字体、字号和字重，并先等网络字体加载完成。
数字有一点对不上
检查缩写规则（can't、won't），以及一个词项在一句中只算一次。
前 1000 个很慢
检查重叠检测是否只看附近的词（第 5 步的网格）。
