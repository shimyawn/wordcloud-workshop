How to read

Two clouds: words used mostly in praise (70% or more, smoothed) and words used mostly in complaints (30% or less). Neutral words such as food or phone are hidden. Size is the number of sentences across all three sources. Colour is the source: if one source gives 80% or more of the word's sentences, its colour; otherwise white. Hover for the counts per source.
